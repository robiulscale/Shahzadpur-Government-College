## 2. Content Gaps & Administrative Action Items

1. **Course Syllabus & Class Routine Downloads (`/menus/3827`, `/menus/3828`)**:
   - *Observation*: The original website menu items for Examination Schedule and Class-wise Subjects contained punctuation placeholders (`<p>.</p>`) without uploaded PDF files attached to those specific menu records.
   - *Resolution*: Current notices regularly publish term-specific routines (e.g. Honours 1st year ICT practical exam, Degree Pass 1st year routine change notice #4032). The redesigned site provides direct links to the relevant examination notices and includes an admin upload tool for the college office to attach master syllabi.

2. **Student List Access & Data Privacy (`/menus/3829`)**:
   - *Observation*: The student list menu item had no public database records attached to protect student privacy.
   - *Resolution*: Public visitor directory does not expose private student records. An authenticated student portal link is provided to the official external system (`https://live.academyims.com/Student_Portal`).

3. **Online Admission Processing Link (`/menus/4060`, `/menus/4061`, `/menus/4062`)**:
   - *Observation*: High school admissions in Bangladesh are centrally processed through the Ministry of Education's XI Class Admission portal (`http://xiclassadmission.gov.bd/`), while National University admissions are handled via `http://app1.nu.edu.bd/`.
   - *Resolution*: The redesigned admission page displays verified admission criteria, eligibility, and direct links to the central government admission portals, avoiding broken fake form submissions.

4. **Fee Breakdown Schedule (`/menus/3830`)**:
   - *Observation*: Specific fee breakdowns are published per academic session via notice circulars, while online payments are routed through `https://pay.academyims.com`.
   - *Resolution*: Provided direct payment portal action and notice filter for fee announcements.

5. **Citizen Charter Digital Text**:
   - *Observation*: The uploaded Citizen Charter PDF (`Citizen_Charter._2404022.pdf`) was encoded with SutonnyMJ legacy font.
   - *Resolution*: The full original PDF document is made available for direct download/viewing, and the key citizen charter commitments are summarized in accessible Unicode Bengali.

6. **College Logo Resolution**:
   - *Observation*: The uploaded logo in `basics` is a low-res scan (`logo_1707237949.jpg`).
   - *Resolution*: Created a crisp SVG institutional emblem featuring the traditional book, torch, Bangladesh national water lily motif, and college name, with an option for the college to replace it in the admin settings.
